1Installation2 minutes

Travely is currently in internal testing and is not yet available on the monday.com Apps Marketplace. Use one of the methods below to install it.

Option A — Install via shared link (recommended)

  1. Your admin will send you a direct install link from the monday Developer Center
  2. Click the link and approve the requested permissions (boards, items, users, notifications, workspaces)
  3. Installation is complete — Travely is now active on your account

Option B — Added as a collaborator

  1. Your admin adds your email as a collaborator in the monday Developer Center (App > Share > Collaborators)
  2. You receive an email invitation — click "Accept" to add Travely to your account
  3. Approve the requested permissions when prompted

2Initial Setup — Policy Configuration10–15 minutes

Getting Started

  1. Open any board in monday.com (or create a temporary blank board)
  2. Click the "+" button next to existing views (Main Table, Chart, etc.)
  3. Search for "Travely" in the apps section
  4. Add any Travely view (e.g. "Employee Dashboard")
  5. Travely will detect that no policy exists and automatically launch the Policy Setup Wizard

Tip: This temporary view is only needed to trigger the setup wizard. After setup, Travely creates a dedicated Travely workspace with all the boards you need. You can remove the view from this board afterwards.

Only monday.com account administrators can configure the policy. Non-admin users will see a message asking them to contact their admin.

Wizard Steps

Choose your setup method:

  • Upload: Drag-and-drop your travel policy PDF or Word document. Monday AI extracts all rules automatically. You review and correct any extraction errors on a summary screen.
  • Build from Scratch: A step-by-step wizard guides you through every setting.

Configure each section:

SectionWhat You Configure
Company DetailsCompany name, base currency (USD, EUR, ILS, etc.), calendar type
Approval ChainApproval tiers with cost thresholds (e.g., Manager up to $2,000, VP up to $5,000, CEO above $5,000)
Per-Diem RatesDaily allowances by country, city, or employee role
Expense CategoriesCategories like Meals, Transport, Accommodation, Entertainment — with optional caps per category
DeadlinesDays allowed for expense reports (e.g., 14 days after return), technical reports, flight booking lead time
Special RulesShabbat/holiday rules, role-based flight class entitlements, laundry/clothing allowances

Review and Confirm:

  • Review all extracted/configured rules on the summary screen
  • Click "Confirm Policy"

4Add Board Views5 minutes

This is the most important step. Each Travely view reads data from the board it is attached to. You must place each view on the correct board.

On the "Trip Requests" board (2 views)

  1. Open the Trip Requests board that Travely just created
  2. Click the "+" button next to existing views (Main Table, etc.)
  3. Search for "Travely" in the apps section
  4. Add Employee Dashboard — where employees submit new trips and track their status
  5. Click "+" again and add Manager Approval — where managers/VPs see pending approvals and approve or reject

On the "Per-Diem & Policy" board (1 view)

  1. Open the Per-Diem & Policy board
  2. Click "+" to add a view
  3. Add Coordinator Workspace — where the travel coordinator manages bookings, tracks costs, and sends itineraries

On the "Receipts and Returns" board (1 view)

  1. Open the Receipts and Returns board
  2. Click "+" to add a view
  3. Add Finance Dashboard — where finance reviews expenses, sees AI-flagged anomalies, approves/rejects receipts, and exports reports

On the "Travely Admin" board (1 view)

  1. Open the Travely Admin board inside the Travely workspace
  2. Click "+" to add a view
  3. Add Admin Dashboard — shows system health, policy summary, adoption metrics, automation status, and links to all Travely boards

6Day-to-Day Workflow

Here's where Travely earns its keep. Once setup is done, each role gets a purpose-built view that makes the everyday work fast and obvious — no training manual required.

For Employees

  1. Submit a trip request: Open the Employee Dashboard view on the Trip Requests board. Click "New Trip Request". Fill in destination, dates, purpose, cost. Submit.
  2. Track approval: The dashboard shows your trip status with a visual workflow stepper (Submitted > Approval > Approved > Travel > Completed).
  3. During your trip: Use "Scan Receipt" to capture expenses. Use "Log Meeting" to record business meetings.
  4. After your trip: When your trip is marked "Completed", click "Submit Expense Report" to send all receipts to Finance for review.

For Managers / Approvers

  1. Review requests: Open the Manager Approval view on the Trip Requests board. See pending approvals sorted by urgency.
  2. Approve or reject: Each request shows policy compliance (within/over budget), employee entitlements, and cost details. Approve, reject (with comment), or request revision in one click.
  3. Delegate: Going on vacation? Use the "Out of Office" toggle to delegate pending approvals to a colleague.

For the Travel Coordinator

  1. See approved trips: Open the Coordinator Workspace view on the Per-Diem & Policy board. Approved trips appear automatically.
  2. Book travel: Work through the booking checklist (flight, hotel, car, insurance). Enter details, upload confirmations, track costs.
  3. Send itinerary: When all bookings are complete, click "Send Itinerary" to notify the employee.
  4. Monitor: Use the calendar view for upcoming departures. Watch for late booking alerts.

For Finance / CFO

  1. Review expenses: Open the Finance Dashboard view on the Receipts and Returns board. AI-flagged items appear in the "Flagged" tab.
  2. Bulk approve: Clean (unflagged) reports can be bulk-approved in the "Clean" tab.
  3. CFO pre-approval: High-value trips appear in the "Pre-Approval" tab for CFO review before booking.
  4. Analytics: Toggle the analytics section for spend charts, category breakdowns, and monthly trends. Export to CSV.

For Administrators

  1. Monitor: Open the Admin Dashboard on the Travely Admin board (inside the Travely workspace). See system health, adoption metrics, and automation status.
  2. Update policy: Click "Update Policy" to change rates, thresholds, or categories. Changes apply to new trip requests only.
  3. View history: See all policy versions and compare changes over time.

8Troubleshooting / FAQ

I installed but see an empty screen
Travely needs policy setup first. Open any Travely view as an admin and the setup wizard appears automatically. After setup, all boards live in the Travely workspace.
I see "Your admin needs to set up Travely first"
You are not an admin. Ask your monday.com account administrator to open Travely and complete the policy setup.
Employees can't see the dashboard
Two things are needed: (1) share the board with the employee, and (2) make sure the Travely view is added to that board.
Automations aren't working (approvals, per-diem, reminders)
Travely's background automations power approval routing, per-diem calculation, deadline reminders, and more. If something isn't firing — approvals not routing, per-diem not calculating — contact support@entrypoint.co.il and we'll check the automation setup for your workspace.
I want to update my policy
Open the Admin Dashboard view and click "Update Policy". Changes apply to new trip requests only — in-flight trips keep their original policy version.
Where are my boards?
All Travely boards are inside the Travely workspace. Click on the workspace name in the left sidebar to see all 6 boards. You can also open the Admin Dashboard view on the Travely Admin board for direct links.
How do I see analytics?
Open the Finance Dashboard on the Receipts and Returns board and toggle the Analytics section.
How do I export expenses?
In the Finance Dashboard, click the "Export CSV" button to download all expense data.
I want to start over from scratch
Open the Admin Dashboard on the Travely Admin board. Scroll to the Danger Zone section at the bottom. Click "Reset App Data" and confirm. This clears the stored policy, board references, and settings — but does not delete the actual boards from monday. After resetting, open any Travely view as an admin to re-run the setup wizard. You can also manually delete the old Travely workspace before resetting if you want a completely clean slate.
